• ROOT CAUSES

Common Mechanical Failure Modes

In Montgomery County, ridge & hip repair issues originate from distinct environmental and structural stressors:

Granule Loss & Thermal Shingle Micro-Cracks

105°F East Texas summer heat bakes volatile asphalt oils out of shingles, creating micro-fissures where water enters under wind pressure.

Rusted Valley Pan Joints

Galvanized metal valley troughs corrode from acidic pine needle decay, allowing runoff to seep underneath adjacent shingle beds.

Popped Fasteners & Unsealed Nails

Expansion and contraction cycles push roofing nails up through the shingle surface, creating small direct water funnels into the deck.

How We Execute Ridge & Hip Repair

Meeting strict Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) windstorm building codes.

01
Targeted Shingle Extraction

Carefully break the thermal seal of damaged shingle courses using a flat pry bar without tearing adjacent healthy shingle fiberglass mats.

02
Decking & Underlayment Repair

Inspect bare plywood for soft rot. Cut out rotted sections, install fresh CDX decking, and apply self-adhering SBS waterproof membrane directly to the wood.

03
Interlocking Shingle Installation

Weave new matching architectural shingles into the existing pattern, staggering nail lines and securing with 6 nails per shingle for 130 mph wind certification.

04
Hydraulic Pressure Water Testing

Simulate torrential driving rain with a controlled water spray test across the repaired zone to verify 100% watertight integrity before signing off.
