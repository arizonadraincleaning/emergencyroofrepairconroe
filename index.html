Frequently Asked Questions About Conroe Roof Repair

How fast can your emergency roofers get to my home in Conroe?
Our average on-site emergency response time is under 45 minutes across Conroe, Lake Conroe, Willis, and The Woodlands. We have live dispatchers standing by 24/7/365.
Will you come fix my roof while it is actively raining?
Yes! We specialize in active storm containment. Our crews deploy heavy-duty commercial tarps and specialized wet-surface hydro-stop sealants that bond immediately to stop indoor water damage during driving rain.
Does Texas homeowners insurance cover emergency roof tarping?
Yes. Under standard Texas homeowner insurance policies, you are legally required to take reasonable steps to prevent secondary interior water damage (known as the "Duty to Mitigate"). Emergency tarping costs are typically 100% reimbursed by insurance.
Can you assist with my Texas storm damage insurance claim?
Absolutely. We prepare full Xactimate damage reports, high-resolution test square photos, and meet your insurance adjuster in person on your roof to ensure every square foot of damage is approved.
How much does emergency roof repair cost in Conroe, TX?
Most emergency roof repairs in Conroe run $500 to $2,000 for a contained leak or minor storm damage. Hail and wind damage repairs typically range $3,500 to $15,000 depending on severity, and a full replacement after a major hail event runs $20,000 to $35,000. When damage is covered by insurance, your out-of-pocket cost is usually just your deductible.
How urgent is a roof leak? How long can I wait?
Treat an active roof leak as urgent. Water spreads fast into decking, insulation, and drywall, and mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours. The safest move is to place buckets under drips, keep clear of wet light fixtures, and call for emergency tarping the same day so a small leak does not become a ceiling collapse or a mold remediation bill.
What is the 25% rule for roofing?
The 25% rule is a building-code guideline: if more than 25% of a roof is damaged or needs repair within any 12-month period, code generally requires the roof to be brought fully up to current code rather than patched. In hail-belt areas like Montgomery County this often means a storm-damaged roof qualifies for a full insurance-covered replacement instead of a spot repair.
What is the average cost to repair a roof in Texas?
Across Texas, the average roof repair falls between $400 and $1,800, with most homeowners paying around $900 for a typical leak or shingle repair. Storm and hail repairs cost more, and prices in the Conroe and Greater Houston area track slightly above the state average because of high demand during spring storm season.
